### Step 2: Apply Shabbat and Holiday Constraints

Before building any search links, flag El Al no-fly days:

- El Al does not operate on Shabbat (Friday sundown to Saturday night) or Jewish holidays.
- Israir also observes Shabbat under current ownership. Verify on israir.co.il.
- Foreign carriers and Arkia fly all seven days.

If the user's preferred departure or return falls on a Shabbat or holiday:

1. State the conflict explicitly.
2. Offer the nearest valid El Al alternatives (one day earlier, one day later).
3. Proceed with the full search including foreign carriers who do fly that day.

Convert using a live Bank of Israel rate from `https://www.boi.org.il/roles/markets/exchangerates/`. [verify] Add the standard ~3% Israeli credit-card foreign-currency fee to any USD fare.

3. **Neither tool available**: do NOT invent prices. Hand the user the pre-filled links from Step 3 and state clearly that live fares could not be read this session.

## Gotchas

- **El Al Lite in Europe/UAE has no carry-on in the cabin.** Since May 2025, Lite fare passengers on European and UAE routes check their carry-on at the gate for free but cannot bring it into the cabin. Only a personal item (38x30x18 cm) is allowed. Matmid Silver and above are exempt. This does NOT apply to US routes.

- **Holiday prices spike before the holiday, not during it.** Rosh Hashana, Sukkot, and Pesach fares typically peak 2-4 weeks before departure. By the time the holiday arrives, prices have often eased. Recommend setting price alerts immediately.

- **El Al no-fly window is wider than Shabbat.** El Al also does not fly on the first and last days of Pesach, the first two and last two days of Sukkot, Rosh Hashana (both days), Yom Kippur, and other Jewish holidays. If a user's desired return date lands on any of these, flag it and offer adjacent dates.

- **Israir also observes Shabbat.** Under current ownership Israir cancels Friday-night and Saturday departures. Verify on israir.co.il before confirming a Saturday Israir option.

- **Date ambiguity in user input.** Israeli users commonly write dates as DD/MM/YYYY (1/4/2026 = 1 April). US-format MM/DD/YYYY would make this January 4. Always confirm the format before building search links.

- **CSV or pasted fare data from Hebrew Excel exports** may arrive in Windows-1255 encoding. If the user pastes garbled text, ask them to re-save as UTF-8 or copy from the browser directly.

- **Mixed currency in the comparison table.** Israir and Arkia bag fees are listed in dollars. Soar MCP returns fares in USD. Convert using a live Bank of Israel rate read this session, never from memory. State the rate used in the table footer.

- **Turkish Airlines and Emirates are not flying TLV.** As of July 2026, Turkish Airlines has NOT returned to TLV (only said "considering"). Emirates withdrew before the 2026 war and has not returned. Do not suggest Istanbul or Dubai connections via these carriers.

- **Foreign carrier status is volatile.** Several carriers (United, Delta, Air Canada, American, British Airways, Iberia) are still suspended or returning only in autumn 2026. Always confirm on the carrier's own site before including it in the board as a live option.

- **Ben Gurion arrival time during school breaks.** The Israel Airports Authority advises arriving 3 hours before international departures under normal conditions. During peak school-break departures (first day of Pesach, Sukkot, summer), add 30-60 minutes.

## Troubleshooting

### Cannot read live fares (no browser or MCP tool)

Cause: the session has no rendering tool and the Soar MCP is not connected. JavaScript-rendered flight search pages return empty HTML via plain fetch.

Solution: do NOT invent prices. Present all the pre-filled search links from Step 3, labeled by platform, with a clear note that fares must be checked directly. Offer to re-run the comparison if the user can connect the Soar MCP or a browser tool.

### Skyscanner returns no results for the destination

Cause: Skyscanner city slug is wrong (e.g., using "athens" but Skyscanner expects "athen" or the IATA code "ath").

Solution: try the IATA code instead of the city name in the slug. Fall back to the Google Flights link or KAYAK link which accept plain city names or IATA codes respectively.

### El Al shows no flights on the requested date

Cause: the date falls on Shabbat or a Jewish holiday. El Al does not operate those days.

Solution: state the conflict, offer the nearest valid date (typically one day before or the night after Shabbat ends), and show foreign carrier and Arkia options for the original date.

### Prices differ significantly between platforms

Cause: Google Flights tends to show direct airline fares; Skyscanner and KAYAK surface OTA deals that may include different fare classes or ancillary bundles.

Solution: click through to the airline's own site to confirm the fare before booking. The OTA price may reflect a non-refundable or restricted class. Always compare the airline's own site price against the OTA price for the same flight.

### User pastes garbled Hebrew text from a copied fare table

Cause: Windows-1255 encoding from a Hebrew Excel or legacy Israeli booking system.

Solution: ask the user to re-open the source file, save as UTF-8, and re-paste. Alternatively, ask them to type the key fields (airline, price, date) manually rather than pasting the raw text.

### Soar MCP returns USD fares but user wants NIS

Cause: the Soar MCP only supports USD output; the `currency: "ILS"` parameter is rejected.

Solution: read the USD/ILS rate from the Bank of Israel at `https://www.boi.org.il/roles/markets/exchangerates/` [verify] this session, apply it to each Soar fare, add the ~3% Israeli card FX fee, and show the converted NIS total in the board. State the rate and conversion date in the table footer.
