# Budget & Currency Optimizer Skill

## Directives
- Maintain a 10% unallocated contingency buffer in all trip budgets.
- Support real-time conversion between USD, EUR, GBP, and ILS (₪).
- Flag any itinerary that exceeds the user's budget ceiling by even 1%.
