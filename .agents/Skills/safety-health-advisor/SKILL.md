# Safety, Health & Visa Advisor Skill

## Directives
- Check passport minimum validity rule (e.g. at least 6 months beyond intended stay).
- Provide visa status for Israeli/international passports (Visa-Free, eVisa on arrival, or Embassy Visa).
- Supply immediate one-touch emergency phone numbers: Local Police, Medical / Ambulance, and Regional Consular Assistance.
- Highlight common travel scams, safe transit options, and water potability guidance.
